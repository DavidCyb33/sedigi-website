SEDIGI / YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy Policy.

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains how Sedigi handles personal information when you visit our website, contact us or request a quotation.

1. Who we are

Sedigi is a UK digital services business operated by David Hewitt trading as Sedigi, who is responsible for the personal information described in this policy.

Privacy enquiries: hello@sedigi.co.uk

2. Information we collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect:

  • Your name and business or organisation name.
  • Your email address and any contact details you provide.
  • The services you are interested in.
  • Project descriptions, requirements and timescales.
  • Correspondence relating to enquiries or projects.
  • Information needed to administer client work and invoices.

Please avoid submitting sensitive personal information through our quotation form.

3. How we use your information

We use relevant personal information to:

  • Respond to enquiries and quotation requests.
  • Discuss requirements and prepare proposals.
  • Deliver agreed services and communicate with clients.
  • Maintain appropriate business and financial records.
  • Meet applicable legal obligations.

4. Our lawful bases

Under UK data protection law, we rely on appropriate lawful bases depending on the purpose of processing. These may include:

  • Legitimate interests: responding to business enquiries and managing relevant business correspondence, where those interests are not overridden by your rights.
  • Contract: taking steps at your request before entering into a contract, or fulfilling a contract with you.
  • Legal obligation: maintaining records where required by law.

Submitting a quotation request does not automatically subscribe you to marketing emails.

5. Services that handle information

Our website is hosted using GitHub Pages. Quotation forms are processed using Formspree, which receives the information entered into the form.

We use Google Workspace to receive and manage business email communications.

These providers may process information on our behalf or for their own operational purposes, subject to their applicable privacy arrangements.

6. International processing

Some service providers may process or store information outside the United Kingdom. Where UK data protection law requires safeguards for international transfers, we will use appropriate arrangements.

7. How long we keep information

Unsuccessful or inactive enquiries are retained for up to 12 months after our last contact, unless there is a legitimate reason or legal obligation to retain them longer.

Client project information is retained for as long as reasonably necessary for delivering services, managing the relationship and resolving relevant issues.

Financial and tax records are retained for the periods required by applicable law.

8. Your data protection rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may have rights to access, correct or erase your personal information, restrict or object to processing, and receive certain information in a portable format.

To exercise your rights, email hello@sedigi.co.uk. We will respond in accordance with applicable law.

You can also raise concerns with the UK's Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

9. Security

We take reasonable organisational and technical measures to protect personal information and limit access to those who need it for legitimate purposes. No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

10. Cookies and website technology

We do not currently use advertising or behavioural tracking cookies on our website. Our hosting and form providers may use technical information necessary for their services, including security and abuse prevention.

If we introduce analytics, advertising technologies or other non-essential cookies, we will review this policy and implement any required consent controls.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our services, providers or legal obligations change. The date at the top indicates when it was last updated.

12. Contact

For questions about this policy or how we handle personal information, contact hello@sedigi.co.uk.
