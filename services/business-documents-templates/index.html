Frequently asked questions

Good to know.

Can you redesign documents we already use?

Yes. We can work from existing documents and improve their structure, layout and presentation while retaining the information your business needs.

Can you match our existing branding?

Yes. Where you have established brand guidelines, colours, typography or logo assets, these can guide the document design.

Will the finished documents be editable?

Editable formats can be discussed as part of the project brief. The format will depend on how you intend to use the documents and the software available.

Can you create recurring reporting templates?

Yes. We can discuss repeatable reporting layouts and, where appropriate, links to spreadsheets or other reporting processes.

Can I download the examples on this page?

No. These are illustrative portfolio previews rather than free document templates. We create bespoke documents based on each customer's requirements.

How much does document design cost?

Pricing depends on the number of documents, their complexity, the required formats and any additional functionality. We'll provide a tailored quotation.
