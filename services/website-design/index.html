Frequently asked questions

Good to know.

What types of websites can you create?

We can help with business websites, service websites, landing pages and redesigns. The right approach depends on your requirements.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Yes. We can review your current website and explore improvements to its design, content, structure and mobile experience.

Will my website work on mobile devices?

Responsive design is an important part of our approach. We develop layouts that adapt to different screen sizes and review usability across devices.

Can you maintain my website after launch?

Yes. We can discuss ongoing updates, improvements and website support as part of your project or as a separate service.

Will my website appear on Google?

We can implement SEO foundations that help search engines discover and understand your website. Search rankings and indexing decisions remain under the control of search engines.

How much does a website cost?

Every project is different. Pricing depends on the size, functionality, content and support required. We'll provide a tailored quotation based on your requirements.
