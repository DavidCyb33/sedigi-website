Frequently asked questions

Good to know.

Do you use AI to create the images?

Yes. This is specifically an AI imagery service. Sedigi uses AI image-generation tools to create bespoke visuals based on your business, industry and requirements, then reviews and refines the results for your website.

Will I need an AI subscription?

No. Sedigi handles the image-generation process. You pay the agreed project fee without needing your own AI subscription, generation credits or specialist software.

Will I have recurring image-generation charges?

No recurring image-generation charges are payable to Sedigi for using your delivered images under the agreed usage terms. New images or work outside the agreed project scope would be quoted separately.

Can you create images for my type of business?

We can discuss imagery for many sectors, including home care, hospitality, trades and professional services. Each project is assessed against your requirements and the suitability of AI-generated imagery for the intended purpose.

Can the images include my branding?

Where suitable, we can incorporate brand colours, uniforms, settings and other relevant visual details. Logos and important text may require separate design work to ensure accuracy.

Are these photographs of real people?

No. AI-generated scenes featuring fictional people are illustrative rather than documentary photography. They should not be presented as actual employees, customers, completed projects or real events.

Can I use the images commercially?

We discuss intended usage and applicable tool terms, licensing conditions and third-party rights before delivery. The agreed usage rights will be confirmed as part of your project.

How much does AI imagery cost?

Pricing depends on the number of images, complexity, required refinements and intended usage. We provide a tailored project quotation rather than requiring you to purchase an AI-generation subscription.
