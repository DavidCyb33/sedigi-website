Frequently asked questions

Good to know.

Do you use AI to create the images?

AI-assisted generation may be part of the creative process, alongside human direction, selection, editing and design. The approach depends on the project brief.

Can the imagery match our existing branding?

Yes. Existing brand guidelines, colours, typography and visual references can help guide the creative direction and refinement process.

Can you create images for websites and social media?

Yes. We can discuss the dimensions, composition and intended platforms so visuals are prepared for their intended use.

Can you edit or improve existing images?

We can discuss suitable editing and enhancement options, depending on the source material, permissions and the changes required.

Can AI-generated imagery be used commercially?

Commercial use depends on the tools used, their terms, the content involved and any relevant third-party rights. We discuss these considerations before delivering work for commercial use.

How much does creative imagery cost?

Pricing depends on the number of visuals, complexity, refinement requirements and intended use. We'll provide a tailored quotation.
