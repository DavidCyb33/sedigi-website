Our creative approach

Designed to build trust. Not just attention.

Frequently asked questions

Good to know.

Do you manage social media accounts?

Sedigi focuses on content creation, design and agreed scheduling. Full account management, including daily engagement and inbox monitoring, is not part of our standard service.

Can I order a single social media graphic?

Yes. We can discuss individual graphics, campaign materials or ongoing content requirements.

Which social media platforms do you support?

We can create suitable content for platforms including Facebook, Instagram and LinkedIn. Formats and scheduling options depend on the platform.

Can you work with my existing branding?

Absolutely. We can use your existing logo, colours and brand guidelines to create consistent content.

Do you use AI to create content?

We may use AI-assisted tools where appropriate, alongside human-led design and review. We consider accuracy, authenticity and relevant disclosure requirements.

How much does social media content cost?

Pricing depends on the type, quantity and complexity of content, together with any scheduling requirements. We'll provide a quotation based on your brief.
