Creative exploration

One name. Four creative directions.

The same name can tell four very different stories.

Typography, colour, illustration and visual style all influence how customers perceive a business. Here we've explored four approaches to the Sedigi name, demonstrating how different design decisions create different identities.

Direction 01 / Official identity

Original Sedigi

Our chosen wordmark. Minimal, confident and designed for recognition across digital and printed materials.

Direction 02 / Concept

Heritage & Editorial

Elegant typography and a refined monogram create a more traditional, established and premium appearance.

Direction 03 / Concept

Symbol & Illustration

An illustrated landscape and natural colours introduce character, storytelling and a distinctive visual theme.

Direction 04 / Concept

Bold & Contemporary

A geometric symbol, expressive colours and strong typography create a modern, technology-focused identity.

These are illustrative creative explorations using the Sedigi name, not commissioned customer projects. Direction 01 represents the official Sedigi identity. Directions 02–04 are alternative concepts created to demonstrate different styles.

Frequently asked questions

Good to know.

Can I order just a logo?

Yes. We can create an individual logo without requiring you to purchase a complete brand identity package.

Can you redesign an existing logo?

Yes. We can explore refreshing or modernising your current identity while considering what your customers already recognise.

Will I receive different file formats?

We'll discuss how you plan to use your branding and agree suitable deliverables and file formats before work begins.

How many revisions are included?

The number of concepts and revisions will be agreed as part of your quotation. Additional work can be quoted separately.

Can you create matching social media designs?

Yes. Sedigi also offers social media design and content creation, allowing us to develop coordinated branded materials.

Can I use my finished logo commercially?

We will agree the intended usage and applicable rights before work begins. Any third-party assets, fonts or other licensed elements will be considered as part of the project.

How much does branding cost?

Every project is different. We'll discuss your requirements and provide a quotation based on the agreed scope of work.
