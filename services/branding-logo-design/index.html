Frequently asked questions

Good to know.

Can I order just a logo?

Absolutely. We can work on an individual logo without requiring you to purchase a complete brand identity package.

Can you redesign an existing logo?

Yes. We can explore refreshing or modernising an existing identity while considering the elements your customers already recognise.

Will I receive different file formats?

We'll discuss how you intend to use your branding and agree the appropriate deliverables and file formats before work begins.

Can you create matching social media designs?

Yes. Sedigi also offers social media design and content creation, allowing us to develop coordinated branded assets.

How much does branding cost?

Every project is different. We'll discuss your requirements and provide a quotation based on the agreed scope of work.
