SEO and GEO: What's the difference?

Both approaches benefit from accurate, useful content. They differ in the types of discovery experiences they aim to support.

Search engine optimisation

SEO

Helping search engines discover, understand and present relevant website pages.

  • Page titles and descriptions
  • Website structure and internal links
  • Technical accessibility and crawlability
  • Relevant, useful content
  • Search performance measurement
Generative engine optimisation

GEO

Improving the clarity and reliability of information that AI-powered discovery experiences may use.

  • Clear explanations and factual consistency
  • Well-organised questions and answers
  • Credible, attributable information
  • Consistent business details
  • Content that answers specific user needs

They work best together.

GEO is an emerging practice rather than a guaranteed route into AI-generated answers. Strong technical SEO, useful content and credible information can support both traditional and AI-assisted discovery.

Frequently asked questions

Good to know.

What's the difference between SEO and GEO?

SEO focuses on visibility in search engines, while GEO focuses on making information easier for AI-powered discovery experiences to interpret and potentially reference. Both benefit from clear, accurate and useful content.

Can you guarantee first place on Google?

No. Search rankings depend on many factors outside any provider's control. We focus on sound practices, meaningful improvements and transparent reporting.

Can you optimise an existing website?

Yes. We can review your current pages and recommend improvements to their structure, content and search foundations.

Does GEO guarantee inclusion in AI answers?

No. AI systems independently decide what information to retrieve, use or cite. GEO can help improve content clarity and credibility, but inclusion cannot be guaranteed.

Do you create website content?

Yes. We can help develop service-page copy, articles, guides and other website content suited to your audience and objectives.

How much do SEO and content services cost?

Pricing depends on the size of your website, the work required and whether support is ongoing or project-based. We'll provide a tailored quotation.
