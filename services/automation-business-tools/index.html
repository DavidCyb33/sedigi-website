Frequently asked questions

Good to know.

Can you improve a spreadsheet we already use?

Yes. We can review existing spreadsheets and explore improvements to calculations, layouts, reporting, data organisation and repetitive processes.

Do we need to purchase new software?

Not necessarily. Depending on your requirements, a practical solution may be possible using tools you already have. Any additional software, subscription or hosting costs would be discussed before work begins.

Can you create a bespoke dashboard?

Yes. We can discuss the information you need to monitor, where that information currently lives and the most suitable way to present it.

Can you automate our invoice preparation?

We can explore ways to reduce manual invoice preparation, including importing records, applying agreed calculations and preparing draft documents for review. The final approach depends on your existing systems, requirements and necessary financial controls.

Can business processes be automated completely?

Some processes can be automated extensively, while others need human review. We assess the risks, limitations and appropriate level of automation for each workflow.

Can the tools work with our existing systems?

Potentially. This depends on the systems involved, their available integration options and your access permissions. We would review compatibility before agreeing the project scope.

Will our business data be secure?

Data protection and security requirements are considered during project planning. Appropriate access controls, data handling arrangements and technical safeguards depend on the type of information and solution involved.

Are the demonstrations connected to real businesses?

No. All four examples use fictional information. Three are limited interactive browser demonstrations, while the invoice example is a read-only illustration. They do not represent existing customer deployments.

How much does a custom tool cost?

Pricing depends on the complexity, integrations, functionality, testing and support required. We provide a tailored quotation following an initial discussion of your requirements.
