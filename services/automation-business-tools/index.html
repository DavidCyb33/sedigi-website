Frequently asked questions

Good to know.

Can you improve a spreadsheet we already use?

Yes. We can review existing spreadsheets and explore improvements to calculations, layouts, reporting and repetitive processes.

Do we need to purchase new software?

Not necessarily. Depending on your requirements, a practical solution may be possible using existing tools. Any additional software costs would be discussed before work begins.

Can you create a bespoke dashboard?

Yes. We can discuss the information you need to monitor and the most suitable way to present it.

Can business processes be automated completely?

Some processes can be automated extensively, while others need human review. We assess the risks, limitations and appropriate level of automation for each workflow.

Are the demonstrations connected to real businesses?

No. All four examples use fictional data and demonstrate possible functionality rather than existing customer deployments.

How much does a custom tool cost?

Pricing depends on the complexity, integrations, functionality and support required. We provide a tailored quotation following an initial discussion.
