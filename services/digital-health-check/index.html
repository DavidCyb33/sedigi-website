04 / Frequently asked questions

Good questions.
Straight answers.

Is the price really £49?

Yes. The standard Digital Health Check costs £49 as a one-off service. Any additional work would be discussed and quoted separately.

Do I need to provide website login details?

No. The standard review focuses on publicly accessible information, so website administrator access is not required.

Is the report personalised?

Yes. The report is prepared around your business and its actual online presence. It is not a generic automated checklist presented as an individual audit.

Will you fix the problems you identify?

The £49 fee covers the review and report, not implementation. If you'd like Sedigi to help with improvements, we can discuss a separate quotation. There is no obligation.

Will this guarantee better Google rankings?

No. The review identifies observable opportunities and potential issues. It does not guarantee search positions, increased traffic or additional enquiries.

What if I don't have a website?

Contact us before ordering. We can discuss whether a different type of digital presence review would be more appropriate for your business.
